Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about how YS Scholaria works.

Which services do you offer?

Career documents (CV, cover letter, LinkedIn profile, SOP), academic and research support (thesis consultation, research proposal, quantitative data analysis, report writing), survey analysis, higher-study support and website design. See the Services page.

How do I request a service?

Use the Request a Service form or message us on WhatsApp. You receive a scope, price and delivery estimate before any work begins.

What should I prepare?

Each service page has a short checklist of documents and details. Tick what you already have and send it with your request.

How long does a project take?

Timing depends on the service, complexity and your deadline. A delivery estimate is confirmed before work begins.

Can I get revisions?

Revision rounds are agreed when the scope is confirmed. Tell us what you want changed and we will work from your feedback.

Will my documents remain confidential?

Your files are used only for the agreed service and are not shared. Please read the Privacy Policy for details.

Do you guarantee jobs, admission or publication?

No. Support improves clarity and presentation but cannot guarantee hiring, admission, funding or publication.

Can you write fabricated research or results?

No. Research support uses transparent methods and real data. Fabricated data, citations or results are never provided.

Do you offer student discounts?

Yes. Student and professional packages have separate prices, and a launch discount currently applies. See Pricing.

How do I pay?

After the scope and price are confirmed, open the Checkout page, choose bKash, Nagad, Upay, Rocket, NexusPay, Cellfin or bank transfer, pay to the details shown and submit your transaction ID.

Is payment automatic on the website?

No. You choose a method, pay to the details shown and submit the transaction ID. Payments are verified manually.

Can I pay by card?

Card and RedotPay payments are arranged privately. Message us on WhatsApp and we will send the payment details or a secure payment link.

Can I get a refund?

You may cancel before work starts for a full refund. After work has started, a refund depends on the work completed. See the Refund and Cancellation page.

Do I need an account?

No. You can request a service as a guest. A free account lets you track requests and payments on your dashboard.

Are the study materials really free?

Yes. The materials in the shared Google Drive folder are free to use. Personal services such as CV, SOP or proposal support are priced separately.
